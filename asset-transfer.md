# Asset Transfer — Federal Lands, Buildings, Infrastructure & IP

**Purpose:** Dispose of everything the old federal state owns — land, buildings, equipment, intellectual property, and critical infrastructure — without creating a successor government.

**Core principle:** _Federal assets are the people's assets. They transfer downward to counties/states, or are sold to fund the debt settlement. Nothing is retained by a central authority._

## 1A. Liquidation Logistics — How the Sale Actually Works

**Core constraint**: No federal sales apparatus exists. The 224-person remnant (federal-remnant.md) has **zero** auctioneers, appraisers, title attorneys, or marketing staff. Counties don't exist as sovereign entities until charters are ratified (Year 1–2). The liquidation must be **county-run from Day 1**, with Transition Trust oversight.

### 1A.2 County Auction Authority (How Counties Sell Before Fully Standing Up)

| Step                                  | Mechanism                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Charter includes auction power** | Model charter template (Transition Trust provides) includes: "County Commission may conduct public auctions of federal surplus property upon ratification."     |
| **2. Interim auction board**          | Before commission elected: **3-person interim board** (Transition Trust appointee + state land office rep + sortition citizen) runs first auctions.             |
| **3. Standardized auction platform**  | Transition Trust procures **national auction SaaS** (Year 0) — all counties use same platform: listing, bidding, escrow, title transfer.                        |
| **4. Shared services pool**           | Counties **pool resources** via compact: 50 regional auction centers (shared appraisers, title attorneys, marketing). Cost: ~$50M/yr total, paid from proceeds. |
| **5. Minimum standards**              | Trust sets: minimum bid = appraised value; 30-day notice; online + in-person bidding; 10% deposit; 60-day close.                                                |

### 1A.3 Unsold Asset Protocols (What Happens to No-Bid Assets)

| Asset Type                                      | After 2 Failed Auctions                                      | After 1 Year Unsold                                                           | Final Disposition                                       |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Surplus land (BLM/USFS)**                     | Price ↓ 20% (min = tax value)                                | → **County land bank** (held for future development, conservation, or resale) | County retains; LVT accrues to county                   |
| **Surplus buildings**                           | Price ↓ 30%                                                  | → **County demolition/repurpose fund** (Trust grants 50% demo cost)           | Demolish or repurpose (housing, civic)                  |
| **Surplus building portfolios** (50+ buildings) | Unsold after 60 days on individual auction                   | → **County land bank** (county assumes ownership at no cost)                  | County assumes title; may redevelop, demolish, or lease |
| **Remote / low-value parcels** (<$10k)          | Bundled into **county lots** (100+ acres)                    | → **County land bank** or **conservation trust** (if ecological value)        | Conservation easement + county ownership                |
| **Contaminated sites** (Superfund, bases)       | Trust funds **cleanup bond** (debt-obligations.md §12)       | → **County brownfield authority** (redevelopment)                             | Cleanup → auction or county retention                   |
| **Specialized facilities** (labs, data centers) | → **State/university compact** (non-cash transfer)           | N/A                                                                           | Compact assumes; Trust funds transition                 |
| **Embassies abroad**                            | → Host country (diplomatic courtesy) or **demolish/salvage** | N/A                                                                           | Proceeds to Trust                                       |

**No asset reverts to a central authority.** Every unsold asset ends up in **county, state, compact, or conservation trust hands**.

### 1A.4 Reserve Prices & Valuation

| Asset Class                | Valuation Method                             | Minimum Bid                         | Adjustment                                       |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Land (surplus)**         | County assessor + independent appraisal (2)  | **Appraised value**                 | -20% after 2 failed auctions; -10%/yr thereafter |
| **Buildings**              | Replacement cost - depreciation + land value | **Appraised value**                 | -30% after 2 failed auctions                     |
| **Spectrum**               | FCC auction comps + propagation modeling     | **Reserve = 80% of mid-band comps** | Standard FCC auction rules                       |
| **Equipment / fleet**      | Market comps (GovPlanet, IronPlanet)         | **Wholesale book value**            | -25% per failed auction                          |
| **Mineral / water rights** | Separate appraisal (if severed)              | **NPV of 10-yr royalty stream**     | Auctioned separately if >$1M                     |

### 1A.5 Environmental Liability Transfer

| Scenario                           | Liability                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Clean parcel**                   | Buyer assumes all future liability (standard deed).                                                                             |
| **Known contamination**            | **Trust funds cleanup bond** (escrow = 150% of estimated remediation). Buyer takes title _after_ cleanup or with bond attached. |
| **Unknown contamination (latent)** | **Trust retains tail liability** for 10 years (capped at $1M/parcel). After 10 years → county.                                  |
| **Superfund / NPL sites**          | **Trust funds full remediation** (debt-obligations.md §12). County takes clean title.                                           |

### 1A.7 Proceeds Flow & Accounting

| Flow                                                        | Mechanism                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Auction proceeds**                                        | Escrow → Transition Trust (daily sweep)                                         |
| **County auction costs**                                    | 3% of gross proceeds (capped at $50M/yr/county) deducted before Trust sweep     |
| **State trust land revenue** (grazing, timber, minerals)    | → **State Sovereign Wealth Fund** (public-finance.md §3), not Trust             |
| **Compact infrastructure revenue** (tolls, fees, licensing) | → **Compact operating budget**, not Trust                                       |
| **Unsold → county land bank**                               | County assumes carrying cost (LVT on itself = 0); future sale proceeds → county |

## 2. Land Transfer Principles

### 2.1 Hierarchy of Disposition

1. **Tribal trust lands** → Recognized tribes (see §6). Non-negotiable.
2. **National Parks / Wilderness** → State conservation trust (no development, no sale).
3. **National Forests** → State trust (timber revenue → SWF; conservation easements).
4. **BLM lands** → State trust (grazing, mining, recreation leases) or county.
5. **DOD lands** → County (base conversion) or state (guard training).
6. **Surplus parcels** → Sold (funds debt settlement).

### 2.2 No Privatization of Parks/Wilderness

- **National Parks, Wilderness Areas, Wild & Scenic Rivers** → State conservation trust.
- **Governance**: State trust board (sortition + conservation experts).
- **Funding**: Entrance fees + state LVT on adjacent land + voluntary donations.
- **No sale, no mining, no logging** (except fire mitigation).

### 2.3 State Trust Lands (BLM/USFS)

- **Ownership**: State (trust).
- **Revenue**: Grazing leases, timber, minerals, recreation → State Sovereign Wealth Fund (public-finance.md §3).
- **Management**: State land board (elected + sortition).
- **Conservation**: 20% set-aside (wildlife, watershed).

### 2.4 Surplus Land Sales

- **Criteria**: Not park, not wilderness, not tribal, not critical habitat, not needed for defense/infrastructure.
- **Process**: Public auction (county). Minimum bid = appraised value.
- **Proceeds**: Transition Trust (debt settlement).
- **Restriction**: Buyer must comply with county zoning + conservation easements.

## 6. Tribal Trust Lands (56M acres)

### 6.1 Non-Negotiable Transfer

- **All tribal trust lands** → Recognized tribes (sovereign title).
- **No sale, no transfer to states/counties** without tribal consent.
- **Treaty rights** (hunting, fishing, water, sovereignty) honored.

### 6.2 Tribal Sovereignty

| Element             | Arrangement                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Status**          | Sovereign nations (domestic dependent nations, as under current law) OR counties (if tribe chooses statehood path) |
| **Jurisdiction**    | Tribal courts (criminal/tribal), concurrent with county (major crimes via compact)                                 |
| **Law enforcement** | Tribal police + county sheriff (cross-deputization compact)                                                        |
| **Health**          | Tribal health mutual (IHS → tribal control)                                                                        |
| **Land**            | Trust title (inalienable)                                                                                          |
| **Water**           | Winters doctrine rights honored (compact)                                                                          |

### 6.3 Federal Trust Responsibility

- **Transferred to**: State compact (if tribe agrees) or direct tribal-federal relationship (Confederation Diplomatic Receipt Office — minimal).
- **Funding**: Trust fund (from asset sales) for tribal programs (5-year transition).

## 7. Territories & DC

### 7.1 Puerto Rico (3.2M)

- **Referendum**: Statehood or independence (binding, 2026).
- **If statehood**: Admitted (2 senators, House seats per cube-root).
- **If independence**: COFA-equivalent (county-to-county pacts).
- **Federal assets**: Transferred to new state/nation (bases → county/state guard).

### 7.2 Guam, USVI, American Samoa, CNMI (~350k total)

- **Same process**: Referendum (statehood, independence, or free association).
- **Federal assets**: Transferred.

### 7.3 District of Columbia (670k)

- **Options**: (a) Statehood (2 senators, 1 House), (b) Retrocession to Maryland, (c) Independent city-state.
- **Referendum**: Binding.
- **Federal buildings**: → DC (if statehood) or sold.

## 13. The Bottom Line

**~640M acres, 270,000 buildings, 17 national labs, 31 GPS satellites, 600M barrels of oil, 90k tons of nuclear waste, and a $1.7T IP portfolio** — all transferred downward or sold.

**No federal landlord. No federal lab system. No federal infrastructure monopoly. No federal IP registry.**

**The land goes to states and counties. The parks go to conservation trusts. The labs go to university/state consortia. The infrastructure goes to compacts. The surplus goes to the debt settlement. The tribes get their land back.**

**Nothing is retained at the center. The center is empty.**
