# Personnel Transition — Federal Workers, Military & Contractors

**Purpose:** Move ~3.5M federal employees, ~1.3M military personnel, ~600k postal workers, and ~5M contractors out of the federal system and into counties, states, compacts, or the private sector — without mass unemployment or institutional collapse.

**Core principle:** _No one is fired. Everyone is offered a path. The federal payroll ends; the skills don't._

---

## 1. The Headcount

| Group                          | Count      | Notes                                             |
| ------------------------------ | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Civilian federal employees** | ~2.2M      | Excludes postal                                   |
| **Postal workers**             | ~600k      | USPS                                              |
| **Active-duty military**       | ~1.3M      | Army, Navy, AF, Marines, Space Force, Coast Guard |
| **Reserve / National Guard**   | ~800k      | Part-time; transition to state guard              |
| **Federal contractors**        | ~5M (est.) | Indirect; contracts end                           |
| **Retirees (already out)**     | ~2.5M      | Pension/annuity (debt-obligations.md)             |
| **Total affected**             | **~12M**   | Direct + indirect                                 |

---

## 2. Guiding Rules

1. **No involuntary separation without a path.** Every employee gets: transfer offer, retraining, or severance.
2. **Accrued benefits honored.** Pensions, leave, and seniority preserved (debt-obligations.md).
3. **Skills mapped, not discarded.** Federal skills (logistics, IT, healthcare, engineering, law enforcement) are exactly what counties need.
4. **Hiring preference for displaced feds** in county/state/compact jobs.
5. **No new federal jobs.** The Confederation has 224 employees total (Mint 200 + Diplomatic Receipt 12 + IDCO 12; the Governors' Nuclear Council is virtual — 0 staff).

## 3. Civilian Employees (2.2M)

### 3.1 Options (Employee Chooses)

| Option                                  | Terms                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A. Transfer to county/state/compact** | Same pay for 2 years, then county scale. Seniority preserved.                                           |
| **B. Retrain**                          | County apprenticeship (healthcare, trades, IT, education). Paid stipend + tuition.                      |
| **C. Severance**                        | 1 week pay per year of service (min 8 weeks, max 52 weeks) + 18 months health coverage (county mutual). |
| **D. Early retirement**                 | If eligible (age + service), immediate annuity (debt-obligations.md §5).                                |

### 3.2 Hiring Preference

- **County/state/compact jobs**: Displaced feds get +10 points (like veterans' preference).
- **Private sector**: No mandate, but county job centers prioritize.
- **No "last hired, first fired"** — seniority preserved on transfer.

### 3.3 Geographic Redistribution

- **Problem**: 15% of federal employees are in DC metro. Counties elsewhere need them.
- **Solution**: Relocation stipend ($20k) + housing assistance (county housing trust) + spouse job placement.
- **Remote work**: County jobs are local, but many are remote-capable (IT, policy, analysis).

## 4. Military Personnel (1.3M Active + 800k Reserve)

### 4.1 Options

| Option                        | Terms                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A. State Guard**            | Transfer to home-state guard. Same rank, pay for 2 years, then state scale.                                   |
| **B. County Militia (cadre)** | Full-time cadre (training, logistics, planning). County pay.                                                  |
| **C. Civilian transition**    | Retrain (county apprenticeship) or direct hire (logistics, IT, emergency management).                         |
| **D. Separation**             | Honorable discharge + GI Bill-equivalent (county education trust) + VA-equivalent (county disability mutual). |
| **E. Retirement**             | If eligible (20 years), immediate pension (debt-obligations.md §5.2).                                         |

### 4.2 Rank & Seniority

- **State Guard**: Rank preserved (federal rank → state rank, same insignia).
- **County Militia**: Rank preserved for cadre; militia members elect officers.
- **No demotion** on transfer.

### 4.3 Specialized Skills

| Skill            | Destination                                                       |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Combat arms**  | State guard, county militia cadre, law enforcement (with academy) |
| **Logistics**    | County emergency management, infrastructure, private sector       |
| **Intelligence** | County sheriff (intel unit), SBI, private security                |
| **Cyber**        | County cyber unit, compact SOC, private sector                    |
| **Medical**      | County health mutual, county hospital, EMS                        |
| **Engineering**  | County infrastructure, private sector                             |
| **Pilots**       | ATC compact, private aviation, county aviation                    |
| **Nuclear**      | State lab compact (weapons stewardship)                           |

### 4.4 Reserve / National Guard (800k)

- **Automatic transfer** to state guard (same unit, same drill schedule).
- **No federalization** (Constitution Art II §2).
- **State mission** (disaster, civil defense) + compact mutual aid.

## 5. Postal Workers (600k)

### 5.1 USPS → County Postal Co-ops

| Element               | Arrangement                                                          |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Structure**         | County postal co-op (member-owned) or private carrier (competitive). |
| **Employees**         | Transfer to co-op (same pay 2 years, then co-op scale).              |
| **Universal service** | County charter requires daily delivery (or 3x/week rural).           |
| **Funding**           | Postage (no federal subsidy).                                        |
| **Pensions**          | Honored (debt-implied).                                              |
| **Facilities**        | Post offices → co-op (or sold).                                      |

### 5.2 Alternatives

- **Private carriers** (UPS, FedEx, Amazon) can bid for county contracts.
- **Cooperatives** (rural electric model) for areas without private service.
- **No USPS monopoly** — competition allowed.

## 6. Federal Contractors (~$700B/year, ~5M workers)

### 6.1 Contract Wind-Down

| Phase         | Action                                                                         |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Immediate** | No new contracts. Existing contracts honored to term (debt-obligations.md §7). |
| **6 months**  | Contractors notified of end date.                                              |
| **12 months** | All contracts ended (except transition-critical: IT migration, asset sales).   |
| **18 months** | Final invoices paid.                                                           |

### 6.2 Worker Transition

- **No direct obligation** (contractor employees are not federal employees).
- **But**: County job centers prioritize displaced contractor workers.
- **Retraining**: County apprenticeship (same as civilians).
- **Severance**: Contractor's responsibility (not federal).

### 6.3 Contractor Companies

- **Diversify**: Defense contractors → county infrastructure, healthcare IT, cybersecurity.
- **Consolidate**: Some fail (no federal revenue).
- **No bailout**. Market sorts.

## 9. Edge Cases

| Question                                     | Answer                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Employee refuses all options?**            | Severance (Option C). No unemployment beyond that (county mutual covers).      |
| **Spouse works for federal government too?** | Both get options. Relocation stipend doubled.                                  |
| **Employee in DC wants to stay?**            | DC statehood → DC county jobs. Or remote county job.                           |
| **Military member in combat zone?**          | Withdrawal plan (defense-lattice.md). Honorable discharge + options.           |
| **Contractor employee (not federal)?**       | No direct obligation. Job center priority.                                     |
| **Unionized federal employees?**             | Collective bargaining honored during transition. Severance per contract.       |
| **Security clearance holders?**              | Clearance → county/state background check (reciprocity). No federal clearance. |
| **Federal employee with disability?**        | County disability mutual + reasonable accommodation (ADA-adapted).             |

| Destination                          | Est. % | Mechanism                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------ | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **County Land Bank / Asset Manager** | 40–50% | County hires from Trust pool; manages unsold surplus, leases, future sales              |

**No one is stranded.** Every role has a clear destination:

- **County land banks** need appraisers, title attorneys, asset managers for the 20% unsold tail + ongoing lease management
- **Compacts** need asset managers for infrastructure (dams, transmission, fiber, rail)
- **State trust lands** (245M acres BLM + 193M acres USFS) need grazing/timber/mineral lease managers
- **Private sector** absorbs auctioneers, marketers, closing agents (booming market in federal surplus)

## 13. The Bottom Line

**~12M people affected. No mass firing. Every person gets a path: transfer, retrain, severance, or retire.**

**The skills are exactly what counties need**: logistics, healthcare, IT, engineering, law enforcement, emergency management, education.

**The federal payroll (~$300B/yr) ends. The county/state/compact payroll picks up ~60% of it** (at lower cost, local control).

**The rest** (retirees, early exits, contractors) **transition out** with dignity and a safety net.

**No one is left behind. But no one is kept on the federal payroll either.** The work continues; the bureaucracy doesn't.
