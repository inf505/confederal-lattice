## The Honest Assessment

**The design is internally consistent** — every "what if" has a cited mechanism. But:

1. **Throughput is untested** — 4 charters/day, 11k personnel/day, 500 consolidations/year
2. **Cash flow is tight** — Trust coupon marginal at high end; severance unfunded Year 0
3. **Political viability is near-zero** — This requires a dictatorial decree; the "what ifs" assume the decree holds
4. **Second-order effects are unexplored** — e.g., LVT on federal land transferred to counties? County LVT on itself = 0?
